I am currently working as a Business Analyst at a GovTech company, where I specialize in creating insightful dashboards and robust ETL scripts using Python and SQL. My role allows me to bridge the gap between data and decision-making, enabling better governance through data-driven insights.

In addition to my professional work, I am actively involved in [SALab](https://salabufmg.github.io) a Sports Analytics Laboratory at my university, with a focus on football. This experience has deepened my understanding of how data can transform sports, particularly in analyzing performance and strategy.

On the technical side, I have solid experience in SQL, Data Science, and Machine Learning, with Python development being my strongest skill. I pride myself on being a quick learner and a collaborative team member, always eager to take on new challenges and contribute to collective success.

My educational journey began with a technical high school diploma in Electronics from [CEFET-MG](https://www.cefetmg.br), followed by my current pursuit of a [Bachelor's degree in Computer Science](https://dcc.ufmg.br/bacharelado-em-ciencia-da-computacao/) at [UFMG](https://ufmg.br). As I near the completion of my undergraduate studies, I am excited to embark on a [Master's degree](https://ppgcc.dcc.ufmg.br) in Sports Analytics at [UFMG](https://ufmg.br), where I plan to further explore the intersection of data science and sports.

Driven by a love for sports and a passion for developing innovative solutions, I aim to fuse these two passions in my career. I am constantly looking for new opportunities to grow, learn, and make an impact in the fields of Sports Analytics and beyond.
